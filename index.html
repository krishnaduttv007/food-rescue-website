Why the sorting works this way

  • Only suitable, safely handled food that has never been served should be considered for people.
  • Food from guests’ plates must never be redistributed to people.
  • Food that appears spoiled must not be given to people or animals. Use an approved compost or biogas route.
  • Food donation and animal-feed decisions must follow local food-safety and public-health rules. When unsure, do not donate it.
Demonstration website with sample partners. Real pickups require verified organisations, safe handling and local food-safety checks.
